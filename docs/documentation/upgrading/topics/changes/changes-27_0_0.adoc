=== Scope mapping listings filter roles by visibility
// https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak/issues/53480

The Admin REST API endpoints for reading the scope mappings of a client or a client scope (`GET /admin/realms/\{realm}/clients/\{id}/scope-mappings` and `GET /admin/realms/\{realm}/client-scopes/\{id}/scope-mappings`, including their `realm`, `realm/composite`, `clients/\{client}` and `clients/\{client}/composite` sub-resources) now filter out roles that the caller is allowed neither to view nor to map to users or to client scopes.
Previously, any caller with permission to view the client or the client scope could see all the roles in its scope, including realm roles without the `view-realm` role and client roles without a *view* permission on the client they belong to.
After upgrading, callers may see fewer results from these endpoints if the scope contains roles that they are allowed neither to view nor to map.
Roles that the caller is allowed only to map to client scopes are returned without their attributes, even when `briefRepresentation=false` is requested.
For example, an administrator with the `view-clients` role but without the `view-realm` role no longer sees the realm roles in the scope of a client, unless they are allowed to map these roles to users, for example through the `manage-users` role, or to client scopes.
With fine-grained admin permissions V2, an administrator with a *view* permission on a client no longer sees the roles of other clients in its scope, unless they are also allowed to view these other clients, or to map their roles to users (*map-role* on the role or *map-roles* on the client) or to client scopes (*map-role-client-scope* on the role or *map-roles-client-scope* on the client).
In the Admin Console, these roles are no longer listed in the *Scope* tab of client scopes and of dedicated client scopes.
