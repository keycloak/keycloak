=  Managing identity providers
[role="_abstract"]

An organization might have its own identity provider as the single source of truth for their identities. In this case,
you want to configure the organization to authenticate users using the organization's identity provider, federate their
identities, and finally add them as a member of the organization.

An organization can have one or more identity providers associated with it so that they can authenticate their users from
different sources and enforce different constraints on each of them.

A single identity provider can also be shared across multiple organizations. Each organization maintains its own independent
link to the identity provider, with per-association settings that control auto-membership and membership type. This enables
scenarios where a single corporate identity provider serves multiple business units, each with its own membership rules.

Before you can link an identity provider to an organization, you create an identity provider at the realm level from the *Identity Providers*
section in the menu. You can link any of the built-in social and identity providers available in the realm to an organization.

== Linking an identity provider to an organization

An identity provider can be linked to an organization from the *Identity providers* tab. If identity providers are already linked, you see a list of them with their per-association settings and options to edit or unlink.

.Organization identity providers
image:images/organizations-identity-providers.png[alt="Organization identity providers"]

.Procedure

. Click *Link identity provider*
. Select an *Identity provider*
. Configure the per-association settings
. Click *Save*

.Linking identity provider
image:images/organizations-link-identity-provider.png[alt="Linking identity provider"]

When linking an identity provider to an organization, the following per-association settings are available:

Identity provider::
The identity provider you want to link to the organization. A single identity provider can be linked to multiple organizations.

Auto membership::
When enabled, users who authenticate through this identity provider are automatically added as members of the organization,
subject to the domain gate (see <<authenticating-members_{context},Authenticating members>>). Enabled by default.

Membership type::
The membership type assigned to users who are auto-added through this identity provider link.
+
* *Unmanaged* (default): the user's account persists in the realm even if removed from the organization.
* *Managed*: the user's lifecycle is bound to the organization -- removing the member or the organization also removes the account.
+
The *Managed* option is only available when auto membership is enabled and no other organization has already claimed managed membership for this identity provider. At most one organization can have managed membership type for a given identity provider.
+
For more details on membership types, see <<_managed_unmanaged_members_,Managed and unmanaged members>>.

NOTE: Setting auto membership to disabled while membership type is managed is not allowed. If you need invitation-only membership, set auto membership to disabled with the unmanaged membership type.

=== Identity provider display settings

Several settings control how an organization's identity provider appears on the login page. These settings are configured on the identity provider itself (at the realm level) rather than on the per-organization link, because they apply globally regardless of which organization is involved.

To configure these settings, navigate to *Identity Providers* in the menu and select the provider.

Hide on login page::
If this identity provider should be hidden on login pages when the user is authenticating in the scope of the organization. This setting is on the identity provider's *Settings* tab.

The remaining two settings are in the *Organization settings* section on the *Settings* tab. This section is visible only when the identity provider is linked to at least one organization.

Hide on login page when organization not resolved::
If enabled, the identity provider will be hidden on the login page when the organization cannot be resolved based on the user's email domain. Otherwise, the identity provider will be shown on the login page regardless of whether the organization is resolved or not. If *Hide on login page* is also enabled, the identity provider will always be hidden on the login page.

Show on login page for unlinked members::
If enabled, organization members can see this identity provider on the login page even when they are already linked to another identity provider.

=== Domain routing

Domain routing -- which determines how users are redirected to an identity provider based on their email domain -- is configured on the domain itself, not on the identity provider link. To configure which identity provider handles a specific domain and whether auto-redirect is enabled, see <<managing-organization-domains_{context},Managing organization domains>>.

== Understanding per-association configuration

Each link between an identity provider and an organization carries its own independent configuration. This means:

* The same identity provider can have different auto-membership and membership type settings for each organization it is linked to.
* Changing the settings on one organization's link does not affect other organizations linked to the same identity provider.
* Unlinking an identity provider from one organization does not affect its links to other organizations.

The following validation rules apply:

* At most one organization per identity provider can have the *Managed* membership type. This prevents conflicting lifecycle ownership where multiple organizations could claim control over the same user's account.
* Auto membership disabled combined with managed membership type is not allowed. Managed membership requires auto-membership because the lifecycle binding depends on the automatic onboarding process.

== Viewing organizations from the identity provider detail page

When an identity provider is linked to one or more organizations, its realm-level detail page displays an *Organizations* tab. This tab provides a reverse view -- from the identity provider's perspective -- and contains two sub-tabs:

Organizations::
Lists all organizations linked to this identity provider, showing the per-association auto membership and membership type settings for each. You can edit the per-association settings or unlink an organization directly from this view.

Domains::
A read-only cross-reference of all organization domains that route to this identity provider. Each entry shows the domain name, the owning organization, and whether auto-redirect is enabled. To modify domain routing, navigate to the organization's *Domains* tab.

=== Mapping federated users to organization groups

When an identity provider is linked to an organization, you can configure mappers to automatically assign users authenticating through the IdP to organization groups based on claims or attributes from the external IdP.

==== Using the Hardcoded Group mapper

The Hardcoded Group mapper automatically adds all users authenticating through the IdP to a specific group.

.Procedure

. Click *Identity Providers* in the menu.
. Select your identity provider linked to your organization.
. Click the *Mappers* tab.
. Click *Add mapper*
. Select *Hardcoded Group* mapper type.
. Configure the mapper:

Name::
A descriptive name for this mapper.

Sync Mode Override::
How membership is managed (Import, Force, Inherit).

Group::
The group to assign users to. If the IdP is linked to one or more organizations, you can select realm groups or groups from a specific organization. The mapper stores the selected organization, so each mapper targets exactly one organization's groups.

. Click *Save*.

IMPORTANT: If the identity provider is linked to multiple organizations, and you need to map users to groups in different organizations, create a separate mapper for each organization. If the IdP is unlinked from the mapper's target organization, the organization group mapping becomes inactive and a warning is logged during authentication.

==== Using the Advanced Group mapper

The Advanced Group mapper assigns users to groups based on claim values from the external IdP.

.Procedure

. Click *Identity Providers* in the menu.
. Select your identity provider linked to your organization.
. Click the *Mappers* tab.
. Click *Add mapper*
. Select *Advanced Claim to Group*.
. Configure the mapper:

Name::
A descriptive name for this mapper.

Sync Mode Override::
How membership is managed (Import, Force, Inherit).

Claims::
Key-value pairs to match claims from the external IdP. Add claim names and their expected values. Users matching these claim values will be assigned to the specified group.

Regex Claim Values::
When enabled, claim values are treated as regular expressions for pattern matching. When disabled, values must match exactly.

Group::
The target group. If the IdP is linked to one or more organizations, you can select groups from a specific organization alongside realm groups.

. Click *Save*.

==== Group selection behavior

When configuring group mappers for an identity provider:

* *IdP linked to one or more organizations:* Both realm groups and groups from the selected organization are selectable. Each mapper targets one organization.
* *IdP not linked to any organization:* Only realm groups are selectable.
* *IdP unlinked from the mapper's target organization:* Existing organization group mappings are preserved but no longer active.

==== Runtime validation

When a user authenticates through an identity provider:

. The mapper checks if the IdP is still linked to an organization
. If the IdP is unlinked, organization group mappings are skipped
. Only realm group mappings continue to work
. A warning is logged to help administrators identify outdated mapper configurations
. Authentication continues successfully—no error is thrown to the user

This ensures that unlinking an IdP from an organization doesn't break authentication, but organization-specific mappings become inactive.

==== Migration considerations

If you unlink an identity provider from an organization:

* Review all mappers on that IdP
* Update any mappers referencing organization groups
* Consider changing those mappings to realm groups if needed
* Alternatively, re-link the IdP to the organization to restore group mappings

NOTE: Organization groups are isolated per organization. If you link an identity provider to a new organization, create new mappers targeting that organization's groups. Existing mappers targeting the previous organization remain valid as long as that link is still active.

== Editing a linked identity provider

You can edit the per-association settings of a linked identity provider (such as auto membership and membership type) at any time.

.Procedure

. In the menu, click *Organizations* and go to the *Identity providers* tab.
. Locate the *identity provider* in the list.
+
You can use the search option for this step.
. Click the action button (three dots) at the end of the line.
. Click *Edit*.
. Make the necessary changes.
. Click *Save*.

You can also edit per-association settings from the identity provider's realm-level detail page. Navigate to the *Organizations* tab and click *Edit* on the organization whose settings you want to change.

To edit the identity provider's own settings (such as display options or organization settings), click the *Provider details* link in the identity providers list to navigate to the realm-level identity provider page.

== Unlinking an identity provider from an organization

When an identity provider is unlinked from an organization, only the association between that organization and the identity provider is removed. The identity provider remains available as a realm-level provider and stays linked to any other organizations it is associated with.

To delete the unlinked provider entirely, use the *Identity Providers* section in the menu.

.Procedure

. In the menu, click *Organizations* and go to the *Identity providers* tab.
. Locate the *identity provider* in the list.
+
You can use the search capabilities for this step.
. Click the action button (three dots) at the end of the line.
. Click *Unlink provider*.

You can also unlink from the identity provider's realm-level detail page by navigating to the *Organizations* tab and clicking *Unlink* on the organization.
